3. Key Architectural Pillars

The four concrete engineering components that make this system work in production.

PILLAR 1
Webhook Ingestion Gateway
Exposes /api/webhook/custom to receive failure events from Razorpay, Juspay, Cashfree, and NPCI switches in under 15ms.
PILLAR 2
Ambient Health Sentinel
Continuously polls bank core sockets (HDFC, Paytm, ICICI, NPCI). Detects latency spikes (>1200ms) and reroutes traffic preemptively.
PILLAR 3
Bandit & SHAP Engine
Combines XGBoost with TreeSHAP for explainability and Thompson Sampling Multi-Armed Bandit to maximize recovery while minimizing processing fees.
PILLAR 4
Cross-Tool Dispatcher
Acts across external systems with zero prompts: triggers secondary bank APIs, sends customer WhatsApp recovery links, and posts Slack alerts.
