1. Ambient Health Sentinel (Sensors)

2. XGBoost + TreeSHAP Diagnosis
Classifies failed webhooks in 12ms. Evaluates 8 transaction features to distinguish transient network timeouts from permanent fraud or stolen cards with mathematical explainability.
3. Contextual Multi-Armed Bandit
Uses Thompson Sampling (Beta Prior α=9, β=1) to select the optimal recovery policy: Instant Backup Reroute, Smart Payday Retry, or 1-Click WhatsApp Link.
